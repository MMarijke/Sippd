## 2. Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel Sippd Pro at any time from **Settings > Subscription** within the App, which opens Paddle's own subscription management page for your account.

When you cancel, you keep full Pro access until the end of your current billing period — you are not immediately downgraded, and you will not be charged again after that period ends.


## 3. Refunds

**Statutory withdrawal rights (these apply regardless of any policy choice below, and cannot be limited by this document):**

Depending on where a customer is located, mandatory consumer-protection law gives a statutory right to withdraw from a purchase, which Paddle honors as Merchant of Record:

| Region | Statutory withdrawal period |
|---|---|
| EU / EEA / UK / Switzerland | 14 days |
| Turkey / Israel | 14 days |
| South Korea / Brazil / Canada / China | 7 days |
| Singapore | 5 days |

This right applies to a one-off purchase and to the first payment of a new subscription (not to subsequent renewal payments). It does not apply once the customer has started using the paid features, if they expressly consented to immediate access at checkout — which is standard for a digital subscription like Sippd Pro, since access is granted immediately on payment.

**Outside of statutory rights — Paddle's discretionary refunds:**

Beyond the mandatory rights above, Paddle may issue a discretionary refund. Paddle's own default discretionary window is commonly 14 days from the transaction date, though this is configurable per-seller within Paddle's supported range.

Refunds are not issued where there is evidence of fraud, refund abuse, or repeated/manipulative refund requests. If a refund is issued, your Pro access ends immediately.

**How to request a refund:** Contact Paddle directly, either by replying to the receipt email you received from Paddle at the time of purchase, or via paddle.net, locating your order using the email address you used at checkout. Please include your order number and the transaction date. Since Paddle is the Merchant of Record and handles refunds directly, this is faster than routing the request through Sippd first.


## 4. Chargebacks

If you have a billing concern, please contact us (see Section 5) before filing a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. A chargeback typically results in immediate suspension of your Pro access while it's investigated by Paddle, and — because a chargeback bypasses both Sippd's and Paddle's own refund process — may result in your account being restricted from future Sippd Pro purchases.
